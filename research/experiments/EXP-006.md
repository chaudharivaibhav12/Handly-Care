## Interpretation
M4 PASSES with caveats: plain English → correct moves on the real arm, 4/4 placements 1-10 mm, relations ("next to", "to the right of",
person's point of view) correct. Confidence: medium-high for the language part, medium for reach (x ≤ ~440 only).
The robot's weak spot is reach near wall-front, not the language. The put-back fix turned a crash into "I couldn't find a safe path",
which matches the "stop when unsure" story. Claude naming is unstable run to run, so safety can't depend on a name; the measured-size
refusals (laptop too wide) don't change. The laptop refusal still needs the real laptop on the table to be tested.
