## Why
EXP-002 failed on finding and sizing (the segmenter returns edges, not tops). DEC-003 replaced it with our own
height map of the full point cloud. This tests that change on the real table.

## Setup
Preliminary run (laptop clock ~2:30): `results/EXP-003-setup.txt`. ANYTHING_SOURCE cloud, CELL_MM 5,
MIN_OBJECT_HEIGHT_MM 15 (raised from 8 after the noise map `results/EXP-003-heightmap-noise.png`),
MIN_OBJECT_PEAK_MM 20, CLOUD_STEP 2. Objects then: red ball, wooden toy, blue foam square (~6 mm), red box at the frame edge.
Final runs (~2:40-2:50): red cylinder standing (ruler 3x3x6 cm), blue cuboid lying (9x1.5x1.5), water bottle navy (7x7x22). White cup (9x9x10.5) was not on the table.

## Interpretation
Positions: excellent (≤ 1 mm spread over 3 looks for everything found). Every object ≥ 2 cm tall was found 3/3.
Heights: within 4 mm for the 6 cm cylinder; +41 mm on the 22 cm dark bottle (tall + dark = worst case).
Footprints: consistently ~+10 mm (edge cells), so fixed by subtracting one cell after this run (untested on hardware yet).
Objects 1.5 cm tall are invisible by design (noise floor 15 mm). Confidence: medium (3 objects, 1 tall/dark).
Hypothesis: PASSES on finding (≥ 2 cm objects) and steadiness; sizes within ±15 mm except the tall dark bottle.
