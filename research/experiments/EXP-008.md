## Interpretation
The medicine demo path works on the real arm: open the drawer, take the item out tilted, hand it over, close the drawer, 4/4 (threshold 4/5 met)
with the red cylinder. Confidence: medium-high for this exact setup (taped box, item on its taped spot, Viam app closed, manual mode off, wire
tied); untested with the real pill box, which may need a re-taught grip. The known risks were not triggered: the cylinder cleared the drawer walls on
the low swing, and the 81° wrist roll didn't snag the wire. The slowest segment, pill_above → high (14.6 s), is the return path, which could be cut.
