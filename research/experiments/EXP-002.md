## Interpretation
Positions are steady (≤ 8 mm) for whatever is found: good enough for picking. Detection and sizing are not:
the segmenter returns mostly 2 cm vertical edge columns (flying pixels), not the flat top surfaces, so
footprints come out 30-60% too small by eye (ball ~50 mm reads 25; blue square ~75 mm missed). A low, flat
object (blue square, ~2 cm) is lost. Confidence: medium (by eye vs the photo, no ruler).
Hypothesis FAILS on "every object 3/3" and on sizes; PASSES on steadiness and on ignoring tray/tape/arm.

## Consequence
- Don't use the segmenter's segments for size. Next: build our own occupancy grid from the camera's full point
  cloud (fit table plane → height map → connected blobs), keep the segmenter only as a fallback.
- Objects for the demo: matte, not black, not clear (rules_out).
- Cord of the tape measure → fine to ignore; MIN_OBJECT_POINTS may need raising.
