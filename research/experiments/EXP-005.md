## Interpretation
Tipping is fixed for this cylinder: 6/6 placements stayed upright with the high grip + 2 mm drop (EXP-004: 1/1 tipped with mid grip + 5 mm).
Tray placement works: 3/3 inside the tray, 8-14 mm from center (threshold 20). This is only 3 tray tries, not the 5 the hypothesis asked
for, so hypothesis "4/5 into tray" is supported but not fully met. Confidence: medium (one object, 3 tray tries).
New hazard: the arm's loose wire swept through standing objects on carry moves (2/2). Planner obstacles + an 80 mm higher carry did NOT
stop it (pick 3); tying the wire did (0/3). The obstacle fix stays (right for arm and camera links, costs ~5-10 s per pick: 42-52 s vs 29-41 s).
The app's own "placed N mm" can read lower than a fresh look (pick 1: 1.3 vs 11 mm).
