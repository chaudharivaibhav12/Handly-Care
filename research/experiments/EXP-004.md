## Interpretation
M3 PASSES: with a calibration spread over the whole table, 4/4 picks landed in the target zone, 6-19 mm from the
target (mean 12 mm). Overall 5/6 grabbed. The one miss was caused by calibrating on the slots only (right side): the
affine (6° rot, 6% scale) extrapolated 40 cm to the tray was 59 mm off. "left" placements land ~2x farther off (16-19)
than "to_me" (6-8): a remaining camera bias on that side. Confidence: medium (one object, two zones).
Open: the first pick tipped a standing cylinder over in the tray (grip at mid-height; 5 mm drop).
